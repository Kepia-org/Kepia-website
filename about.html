About KEPIA

Defending property rights, supporting property owners and promoting responsible property investment across Kenya.

Who We Are

The Kenya Property Investors Association (KEPIA) is a property-focused membership association committed to protecting and advancing the interests of property owners, landlords, tenants, investors, property managers and other stakeholders within Kenya's property sector.

KEPIA provides representation, guidance, advocacy and practical support on property-related matters while promoting informed, responsible and sustainable property investment.

Through professional support, strategic partnerships, education and advocacy, KEPIA seeks to help property stakeholders understand their rights, meet their responsibilities and make informed decisions about their investments.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To represent, protect and empower property stakeholders through advocacy, professional support, information, education and practical property solutions.

Our Vision

To be a trusted and effective voice for property stakeholders in Kenya, contributing to a secure, transparent and sustainable property environment.

Our Commitment

To provide reliable communication, responsible representation and practical assistance while promoting respect for property rights and lawful property practices.

Who We Serve

  • Property owners and landlords
  • Tenants and corporate tenants
  • Property investors
  • Property managers
  • Real estate professionals and developers
  • Families and individuals dealing with property and estate matters
  • Other stakeholders involved in Kenya's property sector

What We Stand For

Property Rights

Promoting awareness and protection of lawful property rights and interests.

Advocacy

Giving property stakeholders a collective voice on matters affecting their interests.

Integrity

Encouraging responsible, transparent and lawful property dealings.

Professional Support

Connecting members with practical guidance and appropriate professional assistance where necessary.

Education

Empowering stakeholders with useful information about property ownership, management, investment and rights.

Communication

We believe that impeccable communication is key to effective representation and strong relationships.
