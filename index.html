Who We Are

Standing With Property Stakeholders

The Kenya Property Investors Association (KEPIA) is an association focused on supporting and representing people and organisations involved in property ownership, investment, management and tenancy.

Property matters can involve complicated legal, financial and administrative challenges. KEPIA provides a platform where members can seek guidance, support, representation and practical solutions to property-related challenges.

Our work covers property and land matters, tenancy concerns, dispute support, property management, documentation, advocacy and member education.

Our Commitment

We believe that property owners, investors, landlords, tenants and managers should have access to reliable information, responsible representation and practical support when dealing with property matters.

Who We Serve

A Platform for Property Stakeholders

KEPIA recognises that different property stakeholders face different challenges.

Property Owners & Investors

Support for individuals and organisations dealing with property ownership, land matters, investment decisions, documentation and property disputes.

Landlords & Property Managers

Practical support on tenant relations, rent matters, property administration, dispute handling and responsible property management.

Tenants

Information and guidance on tenancy responsibilities, rent matters, eviction concerns, harassment, deposits and other tenancy-related issues.

Corporate Property Stakeholders

Support for organisations dealing with commercial property, leases, property management and other property-related matters.

Okoa Maisha

Okoa Maisha is a KEPIA emergency response initiative aimed at supporting vulnerable property stakeholders and responding to urgent situations affecting members and communities.

Through this initiative, KEPIA seeks to strengthen emergency response, community support and protection of people and property during times of need.
