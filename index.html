About KEPIA

The Kenya Property Investors Association (KEPIA) is a property-focused association supporting investors, landlords, tenants, property managers and other stakeholders in Kenya.

Our platform promotes property education, advocacy, networking and practical support on property-related matters.

Our Services

Property Advocacy

Support and representation on civil property matters and stakeholder concerns.

Land & Property Guidance

Practical guidance on property documentation, transactions and land-related concerns.

Landlord & Tenant Support

Information and assistance around rent, tenancy disputes, notices and property-related concerns.

Property Management

Support for property owners and managers in managing rent collection and tenant relations.

Education & Forums

Property education, stakeholder forums and networking opportunities.

Property Rights

Helping stakeholders understand their rights and available dispute-resolution channels.
