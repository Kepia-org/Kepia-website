KEPIA MEMBERSHIP

Join KEPIA. Protect Your Property Interests. Strengthen Your Network.

Membership of the Kenya Property Investors Association (KEPIA) brings together property owners, landlords, tenants, investors, professionals, companies and other stakeholders with an interest in protecting and advancing legitimate property interests.

KEPIA membership provides a platform for accessing property-related information, support, representation, education, networking and advocacy.

Whether you are an individual property stakeholder, a professional, an established investor or a member of the KEPIA Board, there is a membership category designed for your level of involvement.

OUR MEMBERSHIP CATEGORIES

1. INDIVIDUAL MEMBERSHIP

Membership Fee: KSh 30,000

Annual Subscription from the Second Year: KSh 3,000

Individual Membership is designed for individuals with property-related interests who wish to access KEPIA's support, information, education and stakeholder network.

Individual members may include:

  • Property owners
  • Landlords
  • Tenants
  • Individual property investors
  • Property managers
  • Individual property developers
  • Individuals with property-related interests
  • Other individuals seeking support and information on property matters

Individual members can participate in KEPIA activities and access services and support available under the Association's membership framework.

2. CORPORATE / PROFESSIONAL MEMBERSHIP

Membership Fee: KSh 50,000

Annual Subscription from the Second Year: KSh 5,000

Corporate / Professional Membership is designed for organisations and professionals whose work, business or services are connected to property and investment matters.

This category may include:

  • Law firms and advocates
  • Banks and financial institutions
  • Insurance companies
  • Estate agencies
  • Property management firms
  • Surveying firms
  • Valuation firms
  • Construction companies
  • Real estate professionals
  • Property-related professional organisations
  • Other organisations providing services within the property sector

Corporate and professional members gain access to a wider property stakeholder network and opportunities for professional engagement, information sharing, education and collaboration.

3. INVESTOR MEMBERSHIP

Membership Fee: KSh 100,000

Annual Subscription from the Second Year: KSh 10,000

Investor Membership is designed for established property investors and organisations with significant interests in real estate and property investment.

This category may include:

  • Real estate companies
  • Property investment companies
  • Real estate investment groups
  • Property development companies
  • Institutional property investors
  • Companies with substantial property portfolios
  • Other established property investment entities

Investor members have an opportunity to participate in KEPIA's investor network, property forums, education, stakeholder engagement and advocacy activities.

4. BOARD MEMBERSHIP

Membership Fee: KSh 200,000

Annual Subscription: None

Board Membership is a special membership category for individuals appointed or admitted to the KEPIA Board in accordance with the Association's governance requirements.

Board Members:

  • Participate in the governance and strategic direction of the Association
  • Contribute to the development of KEPIA's programmes and initiatives
  • Support the Association's advocacy and stakeholder engagement
  • Participate in strategic decision-making within the Association
  • Support the protection and promotion of legitimate property interests

Board Membership carries a one-time membership fee of KSh 200,000 and has no annual subscription.

WHY BECOME A KEPIA MEMBER?

Property ownership and investment can involve significant financial, legal and personal interests. Having access to the right information, professional networks and appropriate support can make a significant difference when property-related challenges arise.

KEPIA membership provides a platform through which members can access information, support and opportunities relevant to their property interests.

MEMBERS MAY BENEFIT FROM:

PROPERTY SUPPORT

Access to guidance and practical support on property-related matters, including land, tenancy, ownership, documentation and property management concerns.

DISPUTE SUPPORT

Guidance and support when dealing with property-related disputes involving landlords, tenants, property owners, investors and other stakeholders.

PROFESSIONAL CONNECTIONS

Opportunities to connect with advocates, surveyors, valuers, property managers, financial professionals and other relevant service providers.

PROPERTY EDUCATION

Access to property-related educational programmes, forums, discussions and information designed to improve knowledge and decision-making.

PROPERTY RIGHTS ADVOCACY

Participation in KEPIA's efforts to promote awareness and protection of legitimate property rights and interests.

INVESTOR NETWORKING

Opportunities to interact with other property owners, investors, professionals and organisations within the property sector.

PROPERTY FRAUD AWARENESS

Access to information and guidance aimed at helping stakeholders understand property fraud risks and appropriate verification and reporting processes.

STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT

Opportunities to participate in forums, discussions and initiatives addressing issues affecting property stakeholders.

INFORMATION & RESOURCES

Access to relevant property information, educational materials, updates and other resources provided through KEPIA.

MEMBERS FIRST

KEPIA is committed to ensuring that its members have access to a platform where their property concerns can be heard and where they can seek appropriate guidance and support.

Membership connects you to a wider community of property stakeholders while giving you an opportunity to participate in discussions and initiatives affecting the property sector.

Your property. Your investment. Your rights. Your Association.

READY TO JOIN KEPIA?

If you are a property owner, landlord, tenant, investor, professional, company or other stakeholder with legitimate property interests, you can apply for KEPIA membership under the appropriate membership category.
