HOW THE KEPIA BUY / SELL PROCESS WORKS

1. SUBMIT

Property owners submit their property details, while buyers and investors submit their requirements.

2. REVIEW

KEPIA reviews the information provided and may request clarification or supporting information where necessary.

3. LIST OR MATCH

Suitable property submissions may be considered for publication on the KEPIA platform, while buyer requirements may be matched with available opportunities where appropriate.

4. CONNECT

Where there is a suitable match, KEPIA may facilitate communication between the relevant parties.

5. DUE DILIGENCE

Interested parties should undertake the necessary independent due diligence before entering into any transaction.

6. COMPLETE THE TRANSACTION

Once the parties are satisfied with the property and the necessary checks have been completed, the transaction should proceed through the appropriate professional and legal channels.

DUE DILIGENCE IS IMPORTANT

A property being submitted to or displayed through KEPIA does not by itself constitute a guarantee of ownership, title, value, legality, approvals, condition or other property attributes.

Before purchasing any property, prospective buyers should conduct appropriate independent due diligence and, where necessary, engage qualified professionals.

This may include:

  • Verification of ownership and title documentation
  • Land searches and other relevant official searches
  • Confirmation of property boundaries and measurements
  • Verification of rates, rent and other applicable obligations
  • Confirmation of planning and development approvals where applicable
  • Property valuation where necessary
  • Physical inspection of the property
  • Legal review of the proposed transaction
  • Verification of restrictions, charges, disputes or encumbrances

Sellers are equally encouraged to provide accurate and complete information and disclose any known material issues relating to the property.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

KEPIA provides a platform for property information, connections, guidance and referrals.

KEPIA does not replace the role of qualified advocates, surveyors, valuers, estate professionals or other specialists required for a particular property transaction.

Parties remain responsible for conducting appropriate due diligence and making their own decisions before entering into any property transaction.

Do not make payments or commit funds solely on the basis of a property listing or communication received through the platform.
