OUR SERVICES

Protecting Property. Supporting Owners. Empowering Investors.

At the Kenya Property Investors Association (KEPIA), we provide practical support, guidance, representation and advocacy to property owners, landlords, tenants, investors, property managers and other stakeholders dealing with property-related matters.

Our work is centred on helping property stakeholders understand their rights, protect their interests, resolve disputes and make informed decisions concerning their property and investments.

Whether you are dealing with a land dispute, tenancy challenge, property documentation, suspected fraud, rent collection or a broader property management issue, KEPIA provides a platform through which you can seek guidance and appropriate support.

1. PROPERTY & LAND MATTERS

Property and land transactions can involve complex documentation, ownership questions, boundaries, disputes and administrative processes.

KEPIA assists property stakeholders in navigating property and land-related challenges and in identifying the appropriate professionals and institutions to engage.

Our support may include:

  • Guidance on property and land-related disputes
  • Support concerning ownership and property rights
  • Guidance on land and property documentation
  • Assistance in understanding property-related procedures
  • Support in matters involving boundaries and property interests
  • Guidance on property transactions and documentation
  • Referrals to appropriate legal and technical professionals where necessary
  • Support in identifying the appropriate institutions or forums for property-related complaints

Our objective is to help property stakeholders understand the issues affecting their property and take the appropriate steps to protect their interests.

2. PROPERTY DISPUTE SUPPORT & REPRESENTATION

Property disputes can affect income, investments, relationships and long-term ownership interests.

KEPIA provides support to members and property stakeholders facing disputes involving landlords, tenants, property owners, investors and other parties.

Areas of support may include:

  • Landlord and tenant disputes
  • Rent-related disputes
  • Tenancy and eviction-related matters
  • Property ownership disputes
  • Property management disputes
  • Commercial property disputes
  • Disputes involving property agreements
  • Family and estate-related property matters
  • Cooperative and investment-related property disputes

Where a matter requires formal legal proceedings, KEPIA may assist the affected stakeholder in understanding the appropriate process and connecting them with the relevant legal professionals or institutions.

KEPIA's role is to help property stakeholders pursue appropriate and lawful channels for resolving their disputes.

3. LAND FRAUD & PROPERTY FRAUD SUPPORT

Property fraud can result in significant financial losses and the loss of valuable property.

KEPIA supports property stakeholders who suspect fraudulent activity involving land, property documents, ownership records or transactions.

Support may include:

  • Guidance where fraudulent property activity is suspected
  • Assistance in understanding suspicious property documentation
  • Guidance on appropriate title and ownership verification processes
  • Support in preparing and documenting complaints
  • Guidance on reporting suspected fraudulent activities to relevant authorities
  • Assistance in navigating property-related investigations
  • Referral to advocates, surveyors, valuers or other professionals where appropriate
  • Guidance on measures that property owners can take to protect their investments

Where appropriate, stakeholders may also be guided on engagement with relevant government agencies and investigative authorities.

Protecting your property starts with verifying what you own and understanding the documents supporting your ownership.

4. PROPERTY MANAGEMENT SUPPORT

Managing property involves much more than collecting rent.

KEPIA provides property management support aimed at helping property owners maintain better relationships with tenants, improve rent administration and protect the value of their investments.

Property management support may include:

  • Rent collection support
  • Tenant communication and engagement
  • Rent-related follow-up
  • Tenant relationship management
  • Property inspection coordination
  • Management of tenancy-related concerns
  • Guidance on tenancy documentation
  • Assistance with landlord-tenant communication
  • Property administration support
  • Guidance on handling difficult tenancy situations

Our approach is designed to promote professional property management, proper communication and responsible relationships between property owners and tenants.

5. LANDLORD & TENANT SUPPORT

Landlords and tenants both have rights, responsibilities and obligations.

KEPIA provides information and support to help both parties better understand their responsibilities and navigate tenancy-related challenges.

We provide guidance on matters such as:

  • Rent increases
  • Rent arrears
  • Eviction procedures
  • Tenant harassment
  • Security deposits
  • Property damage
  • Repairs and maintenance
  • Water and sanitation concerns
  • Sewage and waste management
  • Unlawful or disputed notices
  • Commercial and residential tenancy concerns
  • Landlord-tenant communication
  • Tenancy agreements and related documentation

KEPIA promotes responsible tenancy relationships based on proper communication, respect for property rights and adherence to applicable laws and procedures.

6. LEGAL & PROPERTY DOCUMENTATION SUPPORT

Proper documentation is an important part of protecting property interests.

KEPIA helps property stakeholders understand the importance of appropriate documentation and, where necessary, connects them with qualified professionals for legal services.

Documentation-related support may include:

  • Lease agreements
  • Sale agreements
  • Tenancy agreements
  • Property-related correspondence
  • Demand and response letters
  • Notices
  • Property management documentation
  • Property transaction documentation
  • Review and understanding of property documents
  • Guidance on documentation required for different property transactions

Where a document requires legal drafting, certification or professional legal advice, KEPIA facilitates engagement with appropriate advocates or other qualified professionals.

7. PROPERTY RIGHTS ADVOCACY

KEPIA advocates for the protection of legitimate property rights and promotes awareness among property stakeholders.

Our advocacy work focuses on issues affecting property owners, landlords, tenants and investors.

Our advocacy areas include:

  • Protection of property rights
  • Awareness of property-related laws and procedures
  • Responsible property ownership
  • Responsible tenancy
  • Fair and informed property transactions
  • Awareness of emerging property issues
  • Engagement on matters affecting property stakeholders
  • Public education on property rights and responsibilities

Through advocacy, education and stakeholder engagement, KEPIA seeks to ensure that property stakeholders have access to information that can help them protect their interests.

8. PROPERTY & INVESTMENT EDUCATION

Many property problems can be prevented through knowledge and proper preparation.

KEPIA provides educational opportunities designed to help property stakeholders make better-informed decisions.

Educational areas may include:

  • Property investment
  • Land ownership
  • Property documentation
  • Land and property fraud prevention
  • Landlord-tenant relations
  • Property management
  • Property rights
  • Investment risks
  • Property transactions
  • Estate and succession-related property considerations
  • Emerging issues affecting property investors

KEPIA also provides platforms for forums, discussions and stakeholder engagement on issues affecting the property sector.

9. PROPERTY TRANSACTION GUIDANCE

Buying, selling or investing in property requires careful consideration.

KEPIA helps property stakeholders understand the key issues that should be considered before committing to a property transaction.

Areas of guidance may include:

  • Due diligence considerations
  • Property documentation
  • Ownership verification
  • Transaction processes
  • Identification of potential risks
  • Engagement with appropriate professionals
  • Property-related agreements
  • Understanding responsibilities of parties in a transaction

KEPIA encourages property stakeholders to undertake appropriate due diligence and obtain professional advice before entering into significant property transactions.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & INSTITUTIONAL GUIDANCE

Property disputes may be handled through different institutions and dispute-resolution mechanisms depending on the nature of the matter.

KEPIA assists stakeholders in understanding the appropriate avenues available to them.

Depending on the circumstances, property-related matters may involve forums such as:

  • Business Premises Rent Tribunal (BPRT)
  • Rent Restriction Tribunal (RRT)
  • Environment and Land Court (ELC)
  • Cooperative Tribunal
  • Magistrates' Courts handling relevant property matters
  • Other relevant government agencies and dispute-resolution institutions

KEPIA helps stakeholders understand where their matter may appropriately be addressed and, where necessary, facilitates engagement with qualified professionals.

11. ESTATE & FAMILY PROPERTY MATTERS

Property can become a major source of conflict within families, particularly following the death of a property owner.

KEPIA provides guidance to stakeholders dealing with property-related family and estate matters.

Support may include guidance concerning:

  • Property belonging to an estate
  • Succession-related property concerns
  • Family property disputes
  • Administration of estates
  • Documentation relating to property ownership
  • Engagement with appropriate legal professionals
  • Understanding available dispute-resolution processes

Where formal succession or court proceedings are required, stakeholders are encouraged to obtain appropriate professional legal assistance.

12. PROPERTY INVESTOR & STAKEHOLDER SUPPORT

KEPIA provides a platform where property investors and other stakeholders can seek information, connect with relevant professionals and raise issues affecting their investments.

Our stakeholder support may include:

  • Property investment information
  • Networking and stakeholder engagement
  • Property-related forums
  • Investor education
  • Guidance on emerging property issues
  • Advocacy on matters affecting investors
  • Referrals to relevant professionals and service providers
  • Information on property rights and responsibilities

KEPIA brings together property stakeholders with the aim of promoting informed, responsible and sustainable property investment.
